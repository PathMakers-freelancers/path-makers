The Solutions page should not be a catalogue of technologies. It should be a problem-solving page.

The visitor shouldn't leave thinking “Okay, they build websites and apps.”
They should leave thinking:

“I have this problem. Now I understand what can be done, what PathMakers would actually give me, and what I should choose.”

I would build the page around this philosophy:

SOLUTIONS
Don't start with what we build. Start with what you need to solve.

Then the entire page becomes a journey.

01 — OPENING / HOOK

Not:

Web Development | Mobile Development | AI | Custom Software

Instead:

YOUR BUSINESS HAS A PROBLEM.
THE SOLUTION DOESN'T HAVE TO BECOME ANOTHER ONE.

Short explanation:

Technology is useful only when it makes your business simpler, faster, clearer or more capable. We understand how your business works first, then determine what should be built, automated, connected or improved.

This immediately establishes the philosophy.

02 — “WHAT ARE YOU TRYING TO FIX?”

This should be the main interactive section.

Instead of asking the visitor to understand technical categories, ask them about their problem.

For example:

“What isn't working the way it should?”

I spend too much time doing things manually
→ Automation / business workflow

My business information is scattered
→ Centralized web application / management system

My existing software doesn't fit us
→ Customization / integration / replacement

My customers need a better digital experience
→ Web / mobile application

Different systems don't communicate
→ API / integration / automation

I can't see what's happening in my business
→ Dashboard / reporting / analytics

My business is growing and our current process can't keep up
→ Custom business platform

I don't know what I actually need
→ Requirement discovery / guidance

And importantly:

Each selection should explain the solution.

Not simply:

"Contact us."

For example:

Problem:
"We're entering the same customer information into three different systems."

What may be needed:

Connect the systems and automate the movement of information rather than building another system for the sake of it.

Possible PathMakers solution:
Integration + workflow automation + centralized data.

That is education + sales at the same time.

This is a very important service.

Instead of making your staff move information from one system to another, we can make the systems communicate directly.

That's a very powerful client hook.
